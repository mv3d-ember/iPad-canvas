iPad Canvas

Share your iPad live to another screen. Peer-to-peer, no sign-up, nothing to install.

Share from this device

You get a short room code, a link and a QR code. Open the site on the other device to watch.

Watch a stream

Type the code shown on the sharing device, or just scan its QR code.

How it works & limits
  • Video goes straight between the two devices with WebRTC. The free PeerJS cloud server is only used to introduce them to each other.
  • iPad and iPhone: Safari (and every other iPad browser, since they all run on Safari's engine) doesn't let websites capture the whole screen. On iPad you can share the live drawing canvas or the camera instead.
  • If you need to mirror the whole iPad screen, use the iPad's own Screen Mirroring (AirPlay to a Mac, Apple TV or smart TV) or share your screen in a FaceTime call.
  • On a computer (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari on Mac) full screen sharing works.
  • Keep Safari open while you share. If you switch apps, iPadOS pauses the page and the stream freezes.
  • Works best with both devices on the same Wi-Fi. Some school or work networks block peer-to-peer video.
